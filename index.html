Shuai Yang

Shuai Yang works on Embodied AI, with a focus on connecting perception, reasoning, and action in real-world settings. He has explored how robots can follow open-ended instructions, understand the 3D world, and perform complex manipulation tasks. These days, he is interested in learning from large-scale robot data and building systems that generalize beyond narrowly defined tasks.
